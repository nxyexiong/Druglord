The source model and textures were modified for Druglord:

- Scaled and aligned the weapon for Bannerlord's firearm animations.
- Combined ten source texture sets into one padded texture atlas.
- Retained a separate barrel material slot for exact muzzle placement.
- Generated a flat normal map for the source part that did not include one.
- Generated three reduced-detail LOD meshes.
- Added a simplified collision body.
- Repacked metalness, inverted roughness, and ambient occlusion into Bannerlord's specular texture channels.
