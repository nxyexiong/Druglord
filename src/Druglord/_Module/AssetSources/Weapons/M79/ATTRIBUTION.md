# M79 Asset Attribution

- **Original work:** [M79 Grenade Launcher](https://skfb.ly/6UL6r)

Druglord modifies the source asset for use in Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord:

- Joined the source meshes and aligned the weapon to Bannerlord's firearm animations.
- Scaled the weapon to 1.2 times its real-world 0.731 metre length.
- Shifted the weapon downward by 0.04 metres after scaling.
- Moved the trigger grip point to the attachment origin.
- Added a dedicated muzzle material for exact projectile placement.
- Generated three reduced-detail LOD meshes and a simplified collision body.
- Repacked metallic, glossiness, and ambient occlusion into Bannerlord's specular texture channels.

No endorsement by the original author is implied.
