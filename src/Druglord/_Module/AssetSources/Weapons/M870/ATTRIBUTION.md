Druglord uses this generic pump-action shotgun model as its M870 visual. The
source model and textures were modified for the mod:

- Scaled and aligned the weapon for Bannerlord's firearm animations.
- Moved the stock-neck and trigger grip point to the attachment origin.
- Retained a dedicated muzzle material slot for exact shot placement.
- Generated three reduced-detail LOD meshes.
- Added a simplified collision body.
- Repacked metalness, inverted roughness, and ambient occlusion into
  Bannerlord's specular texture channels.

No endorsement by the original author is implied.
